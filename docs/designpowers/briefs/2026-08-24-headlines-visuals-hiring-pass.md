## Problem Statement

The portfolio is recruiter-ready when read normally, but a stricter scan still depends on body copy for evidence boundaries, ownership, project state, and some outcomes. A senior recruiter who reads only H1–H3 headlines, metric labels, project images, and visible actions should still be able to advance Hillary for a healthcare Product/Service Design role and understand the hybrid UXR/Service Design lane without overclaiming research.

## Users

- Senior healthcare or health-tech recruiter scanning for 5–15 seconds.
- Product/Service Design leader judging workflow, systems, interaction, implementation, and outcomes.
- Hybrid UXR/Service Design lead judging question, method, changed decision, and limitation.
- Readers using keyboard, screen reader, zoom, reduced motion, narrow screens, Spanish, or dark mode.

## Existing Evidence

- **Home — Conditional:** role, 20% contribution, three outcome titles, and contact are visible; Logistics uses a context photograph instead of a service artifact, and Grove's Home thumbnail is visually low-signal.
- **MSK — Conditional:** the queue/workflow artifacts are strong, but the 20% qualified contribution and sustained-use proof arrive near the end.
- **Medical Logistics — Pass:** the H1, field image, mechanism plates, constraint cards, and outcome field communicate the case without body copy.
- **Grove — Conditional:** eleven-to-three and the screen system are visible, but self-report scope and Phase 2 prototype status arrive too late for a headline-only scan.
- **Opening film — Conflict:** it demonstrates visual authorship but delays role, outcome, and contact for first-session recruiters.

## Approaches Considered

### A. Headline evidence lockups — recommended

Keep the current Riso/artifact spine. Strengthen hero and chapter headline systems so each case makes **Problem · Role · Decision · Outcome/State** visible through headlines, metric stamps, and real artifacts. Replace weak Home thumbnails with existing product/service evidence.

**Strength:** Highest recruiter clarity with the least design-system drift.
**Sacrifice:** Slightly more explicit editorial labeling.
**Accessibility:** Body detail remains in the semantic document; no evidence depends on images alone.

### B. Dedicated recruiter gallery

Create a separate visuals-only route or mode.

**Strength:** Extremely fast scan.
**Sacrifice:** Duplicates content, creates maintenance/claim drift, and can feel like a pitch deck rather than a portfolio.
**Accessibility:** Requires another fully equivalent route and interaction state.

### C. Cinematic visual deck

Turn the primary path into full-bleed screens and motion.

**Strength:** Maximum immediate visual impact.
**Sacrifice:** Hides service/research rigor, weakens direct navigation, and overweights visual design.
**Accessibility:** Highest risk for motion, reading order, and unequal evidence access.

## Proposed Direction

Choose **A: Headline evidence lockups**.

1. Home keeps one role headline, one qualified impact headline, and three outcome-led artifact rows.
2. Each Home project image must prove the work, not only its setting.
3. MSK brings the qualified 20% contribution and implementation state into the opening visual field.
4. Grove makes **34-person self-report · 11→3 decision · Phase 2 prototype** visible before narrative depth.
5. Logistics keeps its current evidence sequence; only wording consistency is checked.
6. Case-study section headlines carry causal decisions, not topic labels.
7. Body copy remains for rigor and accessible depth but receives zero credit in the recruiter test.
8. Default Home entry is direct-to-proof. The opening film remains available only as an explicit user-initiated visual.

## Constraints

- Preserve the existing healthcare-first positioning and Riso system.
- Preserve all research limitations, attribution, ownership, project state, and qualified outcome language.
- Use existing real or truthfully recreated artifacts; invent no screens, metrics, testimonials, or validation.
- Do not copy Carmen's brand styling.
- No dedicated duplicate recruiter route.
- Keep 390/900/1440, 200% reflow, English/Spanish, light/dark, reduced motion, keyboard, and screen-reader equivalence.
- Preserve current unrelated Weekend Journal worktree changes except where the approved direct-entry decision conflicts with the opening film.

## Success Criteria

With body paragraphs ignored:

1. Home communicates role, domain, one qualified outcome, three relevant projects, and one action in 5–15 seconds.
2. Each flagship opening visibly communicates Problem · Role · Decision · Outcome/State.
3. A senior recruiter can identify at least two product/service artifacts—not only portraits, context photos, or decorative fields.
4. MSK's 20% contribution remains qualified and visible in the first case-study scan.
5. Grove's 34-person self-report boundary and Phase 2 state remain visible beside the 11→3 decision.
6. Product/Service and hybrid UXR/Service Design lanes retain 23/23 applicable recruiter checks.
7. A fresh-context senior-recruiter review returns **Advance** using only masked-body renders.
8. Accessibility, research integrity, house style, anti-AI, responsive, test, build, and public-hash gates pass before deployment.

## Out of Scope

- Rewriting every body paragraph.
- New case studies, research, prototype screens, metrics, or testimonials.
- A pure visual-design positioning shift.
- Real-world outreach, résumé tailoring, or application automation.
