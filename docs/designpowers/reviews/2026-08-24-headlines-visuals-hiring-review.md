## Design critique against the plan

The plan's intended scan path now survives without body prose. The strongest evidence is first, actions have an explicit hierarchy, each route preserves its truth boundary, and project-native artifacts carry more weight than explanation. The implementation remains within the Riso system through Compose moves; no new dependency, route, global token family, or persistent recruiter mode was introduced.

Low polish remains non-blocking: Home has three visible hero actions at 390px, the shared case shell creates mild family resemblance, and Grove remains a long case study. These do not obscure role, outcome, evidence boundary, or next action in the final masked or adaptive renders.

## External validation boundary

Real healthcare recruiter sessions, a healthcare Product/Service Design manager review, a UXR lead review, accommodated neurodivergent sessions, and a VoiceOver/NVDA evaluator remain external validation. The portfolio is locally recruiter-ready for the named lanes; dedicated pure-UXR roles requiring several years of end-to-end research ownership remain outside this claim.
