# Louyi 2026 Portfolio Audit - recruiter checklist

Source: **The 2026 Portfolio Audit** by **@louyi.ux**, supplied by the user as `portfolio-audit-2026.pdf`.

Drive source: `https://drive.google.com/file/d/1J9RWLVriIuwzyZGEoJCc09J5OjZgil3L/view`

This is a concise, attributed adaptation for evaluation use. It is not a verbatim reproduction of the PDF.

## 1. Commit to a clear niche

- The hero names one specific role instead of stacking unrelated identities.
- The case studies support a coherent industry, problem space, or capability.
- A new visitor can explain what the designer should be hired to do within five seconds.

## 2. Make headlines carry outcomes

- Every case-study title communicates a result, scale, or meaningful outcome.
- The hero includes evidence of business or user impact rather than only personality.
- Project titles avoid generic “redesigning the experience” language without a payoff.

## 3. Make the portfolio useful in direct outreach

- The designer has a focused target-company list.
- Outreach reaches relevant people, not only application portals.
- The portfolio loads quickly, works on mobile, and places proof near the top.

## 4. Put proof before polish

- Real outcomes appear above the fold or in the first scan path.
- A proof strip, recognizable clients, metrics, or testimonials appears near the opening.
- The proposition would remain convincing without decorative fonts or animation.

## 5. Show decisions, not generic process

- Each case study foregrounds a meaningful decision and its reason.
- Generic process diagrams are removed or compressed to one line.
- Each study is concise enough for a recruiter to scan quickly while preserving depth.

## 6. Pass the six-second test

- A new visitor can name the role.
- A new visitor can recall at least one delivered result.
- A new visitor understands the next action: review work or make contact.

## 7. Keep the public set focused

- Show no more than three primary public case studies.
- Every study earns its place through relevance and credible outcomes.
- Put the strongest relevant study first rather than ordering chronologically or sentimentally.

## 8. Remove next-step friction

- Keep email or a contact action one click away on every page.
- Make the résumé action match its label and the same positioning as the portfolio.
- Verify the complete first-look journey on mobile.

## Scoring

Count one point for each applicable check:

- **20-24:** recruiter-ready; stop polishing by default and focus on targeted outreach.
- **13-19:** a focused repair round can close the main gaps.
- **0-12:** pause broad applications and repair positioning, headlines, proof, and decision storytelling first.

## Responsible-use notes

- Treat claims about the labor market or AI replacing generalists as the source author's opinion, not verified evidence.
- Never fabricate a metric to make a title numerical.
- Preserve accessibility and truthful attribution even when they lengthen a case study.
- Adapt the three-study guideline when private, tailored, NDA, or discipline-specific work serves a clear audience without cluttering the public portfolio.
