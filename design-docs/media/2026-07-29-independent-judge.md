# Independent Judge: Portfolio Evidence Media System

**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Role:** Independent design critic; no production or website implementation reviewed or approved  
**Overall verdict:** **REVISE before production**  
**Review scope:** Frozen brief, copy packet, motion storyboard, visual-direction packet, current repository source and source media  
**Evidence status:** Repository source and media are **Verified as present in the portfolio repository**, not independently verified as research records or client records. Recruiter, cognitive-load, and craft conclusions are **Practitioner synthesis**. Evaluation-matrix source titles remain **Index-only** unless named below as an inspected public framework.

## Summary

The evidence-plate direction is strategically right. It puts proof before polish, protects ownership and project status, fits the portfolio's quiet-authority taste, and can shorten a 30–90 second recruiter scan without creating a fourth navigation path or an autoplay reel.

The system is not ready to produce as currently specified. Grove has materially different stories in the copy, motion, and visual packets; MSK proposes using a dashboard concept that contains invented record-like data despite the packet forbidding it; Grove and Mobbin foreground their methods more clearly than their user problems. The earlier `32/34`, `61%/56%`, and `live/functional prototype` source conflicts are now reconciled in the active source. The remaining issues are repairable specification problems, not reasons to abandon the direction.

No final poster, film, responsive crop, player, accessible description, or compressed export exists yet. Every verdict below is therefore a **production-readiness verdict**, not a shipping verdict.

## Alignment assessment

### Brief

What works:

- The system protects the existing three-case-study scan path.
- Static equivalents are first-class assets rather than reduced-motion afterthoughts.
- Ownership, reconstruction, AI assistance, and project status remain visible at rest.
- The Grove film is user-initiated, silent, short, and non-looping.
- Generative media is prevented from becoming evidence.

Gaps:

- The brief requires recall of the **problem, consequential decision, and truthful outcome/status**. MSK meets that test in the packet. Grove and Mobbin currently make the production method easier to recall than the user problem.
- The brief calls for one frozen evidence story, but Grove currently has two: the copy packet's one reminder override and the motion/visual packets' three examples of five overrides.
- The brief prohibits fake patient data. The proposed MSK dashboard crop includes plausible but invented record-like content.

### Design principles

1. **Evidence before spectacle — aligned.** Real source artifacts, live/vector data, and visible qualifiers dominate.
2. **Motion communicates a decision — conditionally aligned.** The causal sequence is good; the human-override frame is too dense to communicate that decision reliably.
3. **Static is equivalent — aligned in specification.** The posters and adjacent text carry the meaning without playback.
4. **One publication, three plates — aligned.** Shared grid, type, folios, rules, and provenance create a coherent family without erasing project identity.
5. **Truth outranks conversion — not yet fully aligned.** Grove's cross-packet conflict and MSK's pseudo-record crop must be repaired before production.

### Recruiter heuristic

This is a scoped use of the inspected Louyi portfolio checklist, not a full 24-point portfolio re-audit.

- **Proof before polish:** Pass in direction.
- **Show decisions, not generic process:** Pass for Grove and MSK after the specified repairs; Mobbin needs the problem/value line to keep the method from reading as process theatre.
- **Visible result or status:** Pass for Grove and MSK; Mobbin truthfully presents contribution scale rather than inventing an outcome.
- **Fast scan:** Proposed, not validated. No final posters exist.
- **Next action:** The named project CTAs are clear and specific.

## Craft assessment

The visual direction meets the taste profile better than a conventional portfolio reel would. Warm paper, sharp evidence plates, thin rules, Archivo hierarchy, Space Mono folios, restrained painted material, and one coral decision signal express **quiet authority**, **warm restraint**, and **organic material inside a disciplined grid**.

The planned palette is not merely tasteful; its essential text colors are viable:

| Plate | Ink / paper | Secondary / paper | Coral / paper |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Grove | `13.18:1` | `7.09:1` | `4.66:1` |
| MSK | `12.73:1` | `6.92:1` | `5.22:1` |
| Mobbin | `12.74:1` | `6.68:1` | `4.95:1` |

The craft risk is editorial density. A plate can have exquisite typography and still feel unrestrained when it asks the eye to parse six thumbnails, three metrics, three override categories, persistent folios, and a long footer at once. The taste benchmark is an art-book evidence spread, not a compressed dashboard. One problem, one decision, and one status should dominate each first scan.

Texture A is the narrow exception to the no-generation recommendation: as an optional 6–8% material layer it is abstract and restrained enough to support the paper system. Texture B crosses into recognizable repeated botanical decoration and lowers the quality bar. Neither texture is evidence, and neither should motivate additional generation.

## Findings by severity

### Critical

- **MSK pseudo-record data:** The proposed dashboard crop contains invented MRN suffixes, document types, queue count, timestamp, and clinical workflow states. This conflicts with the packet's own prohibition and can be mistaken for anonymized real data. Remove it from evidence media.
- **No shipping pass without rendered evidence:** Final contrast, crop survival, source fidelity, keyboard controls, reduced-motion behavior, captions, loading, and unaided recruiter recall are untested. A production-ready document is not a production-ready asset.

### Major

- **Grove has two scripts:** The copy packet mandates one override; motion and visual direction use three examples and five total. Pick one. The recommended choice is the copy packet's single reminder override.
- **Grove's motion density exceeds its duration:** The third beat carries too many simultaneous claims for 2.45 seconds, especially on the 4:5/mobile version.
- **Grove and Mobbin understate the problem:** Add a literal problem line so recall does not depend on inference.

### Minor

- **Mobbin fan treatment:** `-5° / 0° / +5°` can feel more like a moodboard than an evidence desk and sacrifices edge legibility. Use the smallest rotation that still creates separation; the portrait limit of 2° is the safer benchmark.
- **"Verified evidence" color semantics:** Green can mean source-locked evidence, but it must not imply third-party or independent verification. Labels should name the source instead.
- **Grove caption terminology:** The copy packet calls the on-frame text open captions; the storyboard also proposes a WebVTT track. Preserve both only if the implementation avoids duplicate screen-reader announcements.

### Notes / design-debt candidates

These are not blockers for the first corrected static batch, but should be recorded in the project Design Debt Register by the workflow owner if deferred:

- Test whether the long Mobbin ownership footer remains readable without dominating the portrait plate.
- Test the Grove six-screen contact sheet at 200% zoom and with low vision; keep it contextual rather than essential.
- Decide whether `AI-assisted composite` is necessary when the deterministic plate contains no generated content. Do not imply AI assistance merely because the source prototype was AI-built.

## Persona walkthrough

### Recruiter under time pressure

- **Grove:** Can recall AI breadth, research narrowing, and in-progress status; may miss the plant-care trust problem until the reminder line is elevated.
- **MSK:** Can immediately repeat the problem and mechanism. This is the clearest plate after the pseudo-record crop is removed.
- **Mobbin:** Can repeat scale, method, and ownership; needs one problem/value line to explain why the documentation mattered.

### Product or design hiring manager

- Sees credible decision logic and restraint rather than a gallery of polished screens.
- Benefits from the explicit boundaries: prioritization is not validation, workflow contribution is not sole ownership, and documentation is not product design.
- Will notice contradictions quickly; source and status repair is therefore part of senior craft, not copy cleanup.

### Keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion, low-vision, and interruption-prone reader

- Static-first delivery, adjacent descriptions, user-controlled playback, visible status, and resumable project links are strong.
- The film remains optional and no meaning depends on audio, color, or animation.
- The unresolved risk is reading pace. A technically captioned eight-second film can still be cognitively inaccessible when several labels and source crops change at once.
- Representative testing is still required; this review does not substitute for lived-experience validation or establish WCAG conformance.

## Smallest credible first production batch

Produce **one deterministic static validation batch: the corrected landscape and portrait posters for Grove, MSK, and Mobbin, with their exact adjacent DOM copy and accessible descriptions. Do not produce the Grove film or spend additional Higgsfield credits.** Texture A may be tested as an optional background derivative; the flat-paper version remains the control and fallback.

Why this is the highest-value batch:

1. It tests the actual recruiter promise across the complete three-project scan path.
2. It proves the shared visual system at desktop and mobile before motion adds variables.
3. It exposes truth, ownership, copy-density, and crop failures at the cheapest reversible stage.
4. It gives reduced-motion, low-bandwidth, screen-reader, and interruption-prone readers the final experience first.
5. If the static plates do not improve unaided problem → decision → status recall, the film has no business being produced.

After repair, test each poster with at least five people who match the hiring audience. Show each plate for six seconds, then again inside a 30–90 second page scan. Ask, without prompting:

- What problem was being solved?
- What consequential decision did Hillary make?
- What was the truthful outcome or current status?
- What did Hillary own, and what did she not own?

Advance Grove to motion only when at least four of five participants answer all four questions without the reviewer explaining the plate. This threshold is a proposed production gate, not a measured result.

## Matrix check

- **Applied dimensions:** Agentic UX / Trust; Enterprise Design Systems / Prototyping; UX Research / Strategy; Prompting / Personas / Evals.
- **Hard gates:** Provenance and authority are **conditional**; accessibility and representative evaluation are **not yet passed**; critical source fabrication is prohibited; no consequential external action is involved.
- **Evidence rule:** Repository facts are labeled repository-verified; recruiter and perceptual recommendations are practitioner synthesis; no source claim is inferred from an index title.
- **Validation still required:** final source-owner review, responsive crop inspection, texture-A versus flat-paper comparison, OCR/source diff after compression or upscale, keyboard play/pause/replay, reduced-motion default, screen-reader reading order, 200% zoom, contrast in context, export weight, and representative recruiter comprehension.

## Recommendation

**REVISE.** Keep the evidence-plate system and its visual language. Correct the Grove cross-packet story conflict, remove MSK pseudo-record data, add the missing Grove and Mobbin problem lines, then produce the three static posters as the first batch. Motion may follow only after the Grove poster passes recruiter recall. Generated texture A may be tested as a low-opacity optional layer; texture B and all generative treatment of evidence are rejected.
