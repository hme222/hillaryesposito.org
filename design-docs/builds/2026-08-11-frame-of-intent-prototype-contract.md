## Engineering lenses

- **Gall's Law:** prove one simple DOM/CSS state machine before a WebGL system.
- **YAGNI:** exclude camera and tracking from slice one.
- **Unintended consequences:** separate semantic state from non-semantic deformation; one false ownership inference blocks progression.
- **Testing Pyramid:** focused state/input tests plus real rendered endpoint review; assistive-technology and representative review remain later gates.
