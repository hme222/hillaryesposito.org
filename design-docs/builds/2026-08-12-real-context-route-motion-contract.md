# Real-Context Portfolio Motion — Build Contract

**Date:** 2026-08-12  
**Status:** **Approved by Hillary Esposito on 2026-08-12**  
**Profile:** Prototype  
**Queue:** Feature  
**Recovery point:** current HEAD plus preserved uncommitted Higgsfield documents

## Problem and outcome

The isolated A/B/C lab did not explain how motion belongs in the portfolio. Build one private, real-context prototype where motion clarifies adjacent-project selection and preserves project identity during an actual Home → case-study transition.

## Approved direction

- **Signature:** the selected project artifact survives the route for no more than 560ms desktop / 420ms mobile.
- **Supporting cue:** a bounded paper/light response identifies the active project row before activation.
- **System source:** existing Riso tokens, actual Selected Work rows, native links, lazy case-study routes, and current focus restoration.
- **Motion standard:** Duolingo-caliber cause/effect, anticipation, responsiveness, and resolution without copying Duolingo's brand or illustration style.

## Essential features

1. Real adjacent MSK/Grove/Mobbin rows inside a private noindex prototype route.
2. Pointer/focus/touch selection cue restricted to the active row.
3. Immediate navigation simulation into truthful project destinations using protected artifact identity and normal-link fallback.

## Primary task

Choose one adjacent project, activate its normal route, and arrive oriented to its title and authentic case-study evidence.

## Motion contract

### Row selection

- Pointer intent threshold: approximately 80ms.
- Paper/light patch moves at most 8–12px inside the row and settles toward the authentic thumbnail.
- Title movement: 2–4px maximum; arrow: 6px maximum.
- Keyboard focus receives a fixed canonical state; touch receives a brief selected state.
- No custom cursor, pointer trail, inertia, particles, magnetism, or cross-row field.

### Route handoff

- Navigation begins immediately on click, Enter, touch, switch, or voice activation.
- 0–100ms: selection lock.
- 100–420ms: surrounding paper/type context changes while the protected identity anchor remains recognizable.
- 320–560ms: destination title and truthful artifact resolve; overlay is removed.
- The Home thumbnail may anchor identity but cannot pretend to become a different destination artifact.
- Destination H1 is readable and main focus restoration remains correct regardless of transition support.

### Adaptation and fallback

- Reduced motion: 100–120ms simultaneous crossfade, no travel or scale.
- Save Data, unsupported View Transitions, no JavaScript, late lazy loading, or transition failure: normal route with no interstitial.
- Screen readers receive normal link and route-announcement semantics; transition layers are `aria-hidden`.
- Back navigation restores usable scroll/focus without replaying a compulsory transition.

## Private implementation surface

Use an unlinked, `noindex` route that renders the real homepage work-list composition and truthful destination headers. Do not modify the public Home or public case-study behavior during this proof.

The prototype must demonstrate all three adjacent projects, not only MSK. It may use the existing lab namespace but cannot retain the old A/B/C concept chooser or Playground.

## Constraints

- No Higgsfield calls or credits.
- No generated/altered evidence, copy, metrics, attribution, status, or route.
- No new dependency unless native View Transitions proves insufficient and the owner approves the change.
- No route delay, loading theater, replay control, timeline, player, or instructional panel.
- No public integration or deployment.

## Success evidence

- Real 390px and 1440px renders show adjacent projects, active-row state, transition state, and destination state.
- Normal navigation works with View Transitions disabled.
- Destination title becomes readable no more than 100ms later than the current baseline.
- Keyboard, touch, reduced motion, Save Data, back navigation, and delayed lazy-route loading preserve task completion.
- Five-person prototype target: 5/5 open the intended project unaided; ≥4/5 understand continuity without believing evidence changed; 0/5 call it a loader or AI demo.
- Focused tests, production build, accessibility review, rendered craft review, and blind anti-AI audit complete before owner comparison.

## Engineering Law Trace

| Law | Source status | Evidence and risk | Required change | Acceptance criterion | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gall's Law | Verified | Existing routes and work rows already work; replacing them risks navigation regressions | Layer the prototype over normal links and reuse current focus restoration | Disabling enhancement restores the current route path | Proposed |
| YAGNI | Index-only | A generic transition engine or animation dependency would exceed one proof | Native View Transitions/CSS first; three explicit project mappings only | No dependency or generic motion framework added | Proposed |
| Testing Pyramid | Verified | Screenshots cannot prove focus, fallback, mapping, or timing | Unit-test mapping/fallback; component-test activation; browser-render key states | Focused behavioral tests plus 390/1440 renders pass | Proposed |
| Law of Unintended Consequences | Verified | A visual layer can mask loading, trap focus, or alter public bundles | Private route, `aria-hidden` overlay, bounded timeout, guaranteed cleanup | Transition removal occurs on success, failure, unmount, and timeout | Proposed |

## Stop conditions

- Motion delays or replaces navigation.
- Identity mapping implies one artifact transformed into another.
- Destination H1/focus is not usable within the bounded transition.
- The cue resembles a custom cursor, loader, AI demo, or decorative field.
- Public behavior changes before separate owner approval.

## Approval boundary

Approval authorizes only the private real-context prototype and verification. It does not authorize public integration, Higgsfield production, deployment, or selection as the final portfolio direction.
