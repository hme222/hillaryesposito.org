# Mobbin visuals through the Shape of AI "Synthesis" lens (2026-11-19)

Lens: https://www.shapeof.ai/patterns/synthesis. Synthesis combines many sources and restructures them into patterns or themes, which adds interpretation beyond a summary. Its four design rules: keep reasoning visible; separate evidence from interpretation; expose uncertainty; let the reader verify key conclusions. It names four kinds: aggregated, comparative, thematic, generative.

The pattern is written for AI products. The Mobbin study is not one, so it is used here as a trust test. The job was a thematic synthesis in the plainest sense: 200+ captured screens per app, each labeled by task. The page layers three things on top of each other: the captured screens (evidence), her labels and ordering (organization), and one-line takeaways per app (interpretation). The question is whether a reader can tell which is which, and check any of it.

Study: Mobbin, freelance documentation of three finance apps (Kikoff, Polymarket, Discover), route `/case-study/mobbin`. Source read: `FlagshipMobbin.tsx`, `ScreenSequence.tsx`, `MobbinIndexLens.tsx`, `DecisionStory.tsx`, `CaseStudyCredential.tsx`, `MOBBIN_ES` in `spanishCaseStudies.ts`. The three source images in `public/assets/mobbin/` were opened and compared with every alt text and caption.

Seen live at 1440 x 1000 and 390 x 844 (mobile, 2x), with the "See every screen I captured" disclosure open and the index lens toggled both ways: hero fan of three screens, "One screenshot doesn't show the whole task", the four-step DecisionStory ("Choosing what to keep was the work."), the index lens ("Name the task, not just the app."), the app sequence ("Kikoff shows progress. Polymarket shows the risk. Discover feels familiar."), the Finance+ link, outcomes, close. The Spanish page was checked because its visuals differ (see gap 7). The "What I did" result block another agent is adding under the hero was ignored.

## What already works

- **Scope is stated plainly in the hero.** "As a freelancer, I spent four months on three apps... I took screenshots of 200+ screens per app and labeled each one by task... I did not design these apps or Mobbin." Nothing on the page implies she designed the apps or captured thousands of apps. The Finance+ link says "The library these screens went into", not "my library".
- **The captures are real, untouched evidence.** All three images are unedited app screens (clean 9:41 status bar, no overlays, no added annotation drawn on them). They are framed as phones, not redrawn as mockups. The index lens copy "I kept each source capture untouched" matches what the images show.
- **The index lens is the right mechanism for rule 2.** "Show the task labels" toggles her labels on and off beside unchanged captures. The labels sit on a separate rule below the phones, never on top of the screens, so evidence and organization are visibly two layers. The control uses `aria-pressed` and a live status line ("Three raw source captures." / "Task labels visible beside three untouched source captures."). This is the clearest evidence-versus-organization separation on any case study page reviewed so far.
- **Two of three sequence alt texts are accurate.** Polymarket: "home screen listing live games, each team with a percentage chance" matches the image (Athletics 83% / Yankees 19%, Tigers 42% / Twins 59%). Discover: "welcome screen with a Login button and links to register, contact support, or open an account" matches exactly. The hero alt ("Screens from three finance apps: Kikoff, Polymarket, and Discover") is accurate and the two decorative copies are correctly empty.
- **No invented outcome.** The outcomes section claims only "My screenshots and labels went into Mobbin's Finance+ library." `metrics={[]}`; no usage, search or speed numbers are implied.
- **Type sizes meet the floor.** There is no SVG text on this page (the one `svg` is `RisoDefs`, filters only). The smallest rendered text anywhere in `main` is 13 px at both 1440 and 390 (index-lens labels, sequence kicker, counter "01 / 03", app chips, "Check it on Mobbin · Finance+"), which equals `--ts-label` (.8125rem = 13 px).

## Gaps

1. **The per-app takeaways are styled as evidence, and the screens shown do not support them (high).** The h2 "Kikoff shows progress. Polymarket shows the risk. Discover feels familiar." heads the app sequence, which is wrapped in `data-evidence="true"`. Each slide pairs one screen with a red uppercase kicker that reads like a catalog label but is interpretation: "Credit building · a result after every step", "Betting on real events · odds and risk shown first", "Banking · familiar screens, easy-to-find fine print". What each image actually shows:
   - Kikoff: the welcome splash ("The fastest way to build credit", Get Started, Sign-in). The summary "each step pays you back with a score, a plan, or a sign that it worked" describes a sign-up flow that is not on the page.
   - Polymarket: the home board with live odds and a "Get $20" promo button. The odds are visible; "the rules for how a bet pays out, and the risk stay on screen" is not shown.
   - Discover: the welcome screen. "Fine print is easy to find" rests on a small two-line footer ("Version 2607.0 | Privacy & Terms"), which a reader cannot judge as "easy to find" from one screen.
   Fix: mark the takeaways as her read, not the screens' content (for example a small "My take" cue on the summary paragraph and moving the interpretive half of each kicker into it, leaving the kicker as the factual category: "Credit building", "Betting on real events", "Banking"). If Hillary has the deeper captures (a Kikoff sign-up step, a Polymarket market page with payout rules, a Discover terms screen), pairing each takeaway with that screen would make it verifiable; that needs her assets.

2. **"See every screen I captured" opens three screens (high).** The disclosure label promises the archive of 200+ screens per app; inside are the same three images already shown in the hero and DecisionStory, three more times (index lens, then sequence). This is the single largest gap between what the page claims and what it shows, and the 200+ figure (said three times: hero, Spanish stats, close "After 200+ screens per app") is never shown, scoped or sourced. Fix: rename the disclosure to what it holds (for example "Three of the screens, with my labels"), and state where 200+ comes from (her own count, or Mobbin's count of the published app pages). Needs Hillary's input on the source of the count.

3. **Every screen shown is an entry screen, which works against the method the page describes (medium-high).** Step 02 says "Marketing welcome screens didn't get the same space as signing up, paying, managing an account" and "Real tasks in, splash screens out". Yet two of the three evidence images are welcome screens (Kikoff splash, Discover welcome), and the index lens itself tags them "Entry · credit building" and "Entry · account access". During DecisionStory step 01 ("Screenshot the whole task... the screens that only make sense in order") the frame shows the Kikoff splash alone; no sequence is ever shown. The thematic rule "show the logic" cannot be met with one screen per app: a reader never sees a task captured "in order, not a pile". Fix: if real captures exist, show one short ordered flow (for example 4 to 6 screens of one task in one app) with her labels under each; this is the evidence the decisions describe. Needs her assets. Without them, say on the page that these three are entry screens chosen as samples.

4. **The label vocabulary cannot be checked (medium).** The lens says the labels use "the library's vocabulary" and step 03 says "Use Mobbin's words". The three tags ("Entry · credit building", "Browse · live markets", "Entry · account access") are the only labels on the page, and nothing tells the reader whether they are the labels she submitted, Mobbin's category names, or a portfolio reconstruction. The step note "Mobbin's words meant fewer redos" is a result claim with no source. Fix: one line under the lens saying what the tags are (submitted labels, or reconstructed for this page); and either source "fewer redos" (how she knows) or mark it as her recollection. Needs Hillary's input.

5. **Verification is one generic link, and there is no client quote or credential on this page (medium).** The brief for this review mentions a client quote/credential; neither exists on the English or Spanish Mobbin page (`CaseStudyCredential` is used only on Knowunity). Rule 4 rests entirely on "Check it on Mobbin · Finance+", which points at `https://mobbin.com/finance`, a category landing page, not the three apps' pages, and sits inside the collapsed disclosure at the bottom. Fix: link directly to the Kikoff, Polymarket and Discover pages on Mobbin if they are public (needs Hillary to supply the URLs and say whether Finance+ is behind a paywall), and lift the link out of the disclosure next to the outcome line "My screenshots and labels went into Mobbin's Finance+ library." If she has a client reference she is allowed to show, that would be the stronger check; do not add one without it.

6. **Kikoff alt text and the lens alts do not describe the image (low-medium).** "Kikoff credit-building app screen, one of the screens I captured" hides that it is the welcome splash, which matters because the summary beside it describes later steps. The three index-lens alts ("Kikoff interface documented for Mobbin", etc.) say nothing about content and repeat for each app. Fix: Kikoff: "Kikoff welcome screen: 'The fastest way to build credit', a Get Started button and a sign-in link." Lens images can reuse the accurate sequence alts.

7. **The Spanish page has no evidence at all (medium).** `MOBBIN_ES` renders text only: no captures, no index lens, and no Finance+ link, although it says "Las pantallas están en Mobbin Finance+, la biblioteca a la que se integraron." The Spanish stats repeat "más de 200 pantallas cada una" with nothing to see. Fix: at minimum add the same Finance+ link to the Spanish "Apps" or "Resultados" section; ideally the three captures with the Spanish alt text.
