## Accessibility and neurodiversity review

- **Perceivable:** the vehicle is decorative and `aria-hidden`; attribution and the issue label are live text. Meaning does not depend on the blue/orange lines.
- **Operable:** the existing native button remains the only control, retains its 48px target, focus behavior, `aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls` contract.
- **Predictability:** arrival follows the user's scroll, clamps at both ends, parks, and departs only when the journal opens. There is no loop, sound, timeout, hover requirement, or content delay.
- **Sensory regulation:** reduced motion removes the vehicle and presents the delivered label immediately. Scroll interruption leaves a stable state.
- **Cognitive load:** the resting journal still exposes one question and one action. The train adds no new choice or instruction.
- **Evidence status:** behavior and geometry are Verified in source/tests/browser captures; COGA framing is standards-based supplemental guidance. Lived-experience validation remains required before condition-specific claims.
