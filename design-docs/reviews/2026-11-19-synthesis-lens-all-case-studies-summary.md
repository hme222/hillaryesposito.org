# Synthesis lens across all six case studies (2026-11-19)

Lens: Shape of AI "Synthesis" (shapeof.ai/patterns/synthesis), used as a trust test: keep reasoning visible; separate evidence from interpretation; expose uncertainty; let the reader verify. Individual reviews: `2026-11-19-{msk,uxwoman,knowunity,grove,logistics,mobbin}-synthesis-lens-review.md`.

## Patterns that repeat across studies

1. **Opinion styled as evidence.** Logistics (outcome tags name a cause, not the kind of claim), Mobbin (per-app takeaways under the "evidence" viewer), Knowunity ("Proof, not praise" states an untested guess as a verdict), UX Woman (themes 5 and 6 tagged AI but the heading says "six themes I heard"). MSK had it and is fixed.
2. **Visuals that show something other than what the text describes, with no caption.** Knowunity (staged results on the hero and decision phones), FloraGrove (the demo video shows a build with no feed or mission and parts of the planned redesign), Mobbin (only entry screens, though step 02 says splash screens were cut), Logistics (small drawings with no "schematic" label).
3. **Numbers without a unit, source or limit.** FloraGrove (percentages sum to about 300% and say nothing about "of 37"), Logistics (85% faster is more exact than 1-2 months to 1-2 weeks allows; 15% has no interview record), UX Woman (18,164 and the policy dates have no source), Mobbin ("200+ screens" never shown or sourced).
4. **A claim with nothing behind it.** Knowunity ("Knowunity reviewed it" in the outcomes headline), Mobbin ("Mobbin's words meant fewer redos").
5. **Text inside images below the 13 px floor.** Knowunity phone screens (about 4-7 px), FloraGrove video (about 5-9 px), UX Woman sketches (about 3-9 px), Logistics drawings (8-10.7 px). MSK now at 12.5 px or more.
6. **Spanish editions drop the visuals and sometimes the safeguards.** Mobbin (no screens, no Finance+ link), FloraGrove (no visuals, no "of 37"), UX Woman (lists artifacts the English page never shows), Knowunity (the Spanish page marks decisions "untested"; the English page does not).
