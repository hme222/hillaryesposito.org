# Higgsfield two-concept panel review

**Date:** 2026-08-12  
**Reviewer:** accessibility-reviewer acting as adversarial recruiter and design critic  
**Concepts reviewed:** `The Work Has Weather` and `The Page Remembers`  
**Authority:** approved reset brief, innovation scout, current project state, repository artifacts, and current Riso implementation  
**Review type:** concept/specification review. No generated media, route prototype, rendered adaptive states, network trace, assistive-technology session, or participant result exists yet.

## Verdict

**Both concepts are worthy of an owner choice, but neither should be presented as implementation-ready or authorised for paid generation.** They are materially different: A changes the atmosphere of the homepage scan; B changes the transition after a visitor chooses a project. B is the stronger and safer recommendation because its surprise is caused by user intent, leaves the first scan quiet, adds no persistent object, and has tighter performance and motion limits.

The owner choice should be framed as:

- **Preferred: The Page Remembers — 86/100, Revise before proof.** Preserve it if the desired signature is one causal interaction at the exact moment a recruiter commits to the work. Fix the false MSK object-continuity premise, remove focus-triggered media fetching, and restore the scout's three-generation ceiling.
- **Viable challenger: The Work Has Weather — 71/100, Revise before proof.** Preserve it if the owner values a memorable homepage atmosphere enough to accept a higher anti-AI, attention, motion, and performance burden. Fix the MSK metaphor/evidence mismatch, make static art direction the complete default state, sharply reduce the media budget, and add a hard three-generation ceiling.

Do not choose from isolated Higgsfield clips. Choose from a randomized, no-credit A/B/current prototype in the real homepage and real routes.

### Hard blockers

Any one of these returns the concept to **Revise**, regardless of score:

1. **Evidence integrity:** any protected artifact, UI, label, metric, attribution, ownership statement, or status enters a generator, changes in the composite, becomes obscured, or is linked by a false before/after or same-object claim.
2. **Static recruiter path:** a visitor cannot identify and open the intended case study inside ten seconds on first paint, without waiting for media or learning a behavior.
3. **Critical access and recovery:** the real link, visible focus, route announcement, focus restoration, reflow, zoom, reduced-motion, Save-Data, no-media, media-failure, or no-JavaScript path is incomplete or loses meaning.
4. **Performance:** homepage LCP or readable destination title is delayed by generated media; route-to-readable-title exceeds the current cache-cold mobile baseline by more than 100ms; late decode cannot cut directly to the destination.
5. **False authorship or rejected grammar:** participants infer that Hillary designed third-party Mobbin products, generated product evidence, shipped unfinished Grove work, or built a poster/player/dashboard/explainer/AI demo.
6. **Unbounded production:** a proof exceeds three paid generations, uses a capability or entitlement not reverified against the live account, or uploads protected institutional or third-party material.

## Concept A: The Work Has Weather

### Summary

**Status: Revise and re-review.** It has the higher screenshot-level ceiling and is genuinely unlike the rejected poster and explainer UI. It also carries the highest risk of turning the portfolio into an ambient AI art reel, distracting during the exact scan the brief protects, and spending several megabytes to create mood rather than clearer proof.

### Critical issues

No confirmed critical access failure appears in the written fallback contract. Critical-flow conformance remains **unverified**, not passed, until a rendered prototype is tested with keyboard, screen reader, zoom, reduced motion, Save-Data, no media, failed media, and no JavaScript.

### Major issues

1. **The MSK environment claims alignment that the proposed homepage map crop does not prove.** The location/map thumbnail is authentic, but it is not the recreated workflow artifact behind “four systems became two.” A slow room resolving four paths into two can make atmosphere perform as evidence. This affects skeptical design leaders, clinical-domain reviewers, and anyone sensitive to fabricated causality. **Fix:** use a deterministic export of `MSKWorkflowMap` as the foreground for this expression, or remove the four-to-two alignment action and use a neutral handoff/material behavior. Do not pair the location crop with a systems-consolidation metaphor. **Verify:** 0/5 participants infer that the map image itself documents the workflow redesign.

2. **Three 3.2-second scroll/focus-triggered viewport fields compete with reading.** This can repeatedly seize attention for people with vestibular sensitivity, ADHD, migraine, cognitive fatigue, or situational interruption. It also conflicts with the existing direction lock's restrained motion budget unless the owner explicitly approves an exception. **Fix:** make each art-directed final still a complete state; play at most one active field at a time, once per page visit, only when standard motion is allowed, and never restart on re-entry or focus cycling. Record any duration over the current 250ms house-style budget as an owner-approved exception. **Verify:** no discomfort; no reading interruption; no replay on reverse scroll, focus return, language switch, or browser back.

3. **The production weight is disproportionate to recruiter value.** Three roughly 1MB files can add about 3MB of decorative media and decode pressure to the homepage. One-active-at-a-time does not remove transfer cost if each is requested during a short scan. This affects low-data visitors, constrained mobile devices, battery-limited users, and recruiters on managed networks. **Fix:** no generated request before core content, fonts, and authentic thumbnails are stable; hard ceiling **400KB for the single requested asset**, no speculative loading of all rows, and still-only shipping when that ceiling harms quality. **Verify:** LCP unchanged within test tolerance, zero CLS, no long task above 50ms, and no request under Save-Data or reduced motion.

4. **The environments use an AI-familiar semantic shorthand.** Clinical corridors, a greenhouse exhaling, botanical shadows, and archive depth are polished but probable-token choices. Grove is especially close to generic plant-brand AI luxury; Mobbin's immersive archive can make third-party screens feel like Hillary's designed product world. **Fix:** remove room/set language from the production frames; base each visual behavior on one observable property of the real artifact, keep Mobbin's ownership line in live text within the same viewport, and reject any frame participants call “AI,” “ad,” “showreel,” “museum,” or “brand film.” **Verify:** 0/5 false ownership inferences and at least 4/5 remember a real project distinction rather than the generated set.

5. **Replacement safety is geometric, not yet semantic.** `object-fit: contain` and safe apertures prevent crop damage, but a valid new artifact may no longer justify the room, lighting, or metaphor. This affects maintainers and future recruiter trust. **Fix:** add a replacement test with one deliberately mismatched aspect ratio and one semantically different approved artifact; disable the atmosphere when either the protected safe zone or the project-specific metaphor no longer holds. **Verify:** no regeneration and no loss of project specificity.

6. **The credit stop is not hard enough.** “One four-variation batch,” “two failed motion rounds,” and three final environments do not enforce the scout's maximum of three paid generations. A also names `Cinema Studio 3.5` and `3D Scene / Enter the Set`, while the supplied scout verifies a Cinema Studio guide, start/end controls, and After Effects compositing but does not verify those exact labels or account entitlements. **Fix:** maximum three paid generations total for the proof, record the displayed cost before each click, and reverify the exact feature, commercial-use, privacy/retention, and account entitlement before production.

### Minor issues

1. **The still and no-media variants need an explicit hierarchy contract.** Generated fields are correctly decorative, but low contrast can still reduce legibility behind live text. This affects low-vision users and people using custom colour settings. **Fix:** atmosphere must sit outside text/focus zones or pass the same contrast measurements in every still; omit it entirely in forced colours and at 200–400% zoom. **Verify:** measured contrast, text-spacing tests, and no overlap at 320px.

2. **Localized geometry is unspecified.** English copy is promised unchanged, but Spanish rows can be taller and alter the active viewport band. This affects multilingual readers and zoom users. **Fix:** derive activation from the live row bounds after fonts and translation settle; never bind it to English heights. **Verify:** Spanish at 320px, 400% zoom, orientation change, and browser back.

### What works well

- The current Selected Work structure, project order, reading length, native routes, and live qualifiers remain intact.
- Protected evidence never enters Higgsfield; the generator receives only source-free matter and light.
- Mobile, reduced-motion, Save-Data, no-media, no-JavaScript, zoom, and forced-colour fallbacks are treated as complete states, not error messages.
- MSK-first proof before paid generation is the right cost order.
- The concept is materially different from B and from the rejected poster/explainer structures.

## Concept B: The Page Remembers

### Summary

**Status: Revise and re-review; preferred for owner selection.** The interaction has a clear cause: the visitor chose real work, so the publication preserves that object while changing context. It keeps the homepage quiet and makes AI nearly invisible. Its central promise depends on real one-to-one source/destination artifact identity, and that mapping is not currently true for MSK.

### Critical issues

No confirmed critical access failure appears in the specification. The current MSK identity mismatch is a **hard truth blocker**, not a WCAG failure: it must be fixed before a proof can claim that the same object persists.

### Major issues

1. **MSK does not currently have the promised same-object landing.** Home uses `mskcc-map-thumb.jpg`; the case-study hero uses a larger map field plus a recreated dashboard, while the consequential workflow artifact appears later. The proposal itself says the crop settles “beside” the hero and must not claim that the location image proves the workflow redesign. That is honest, but it contradicts “the exact same artifact resolves into its case-study hero or first evidence position.” This affects skeptical recruiters, design leaders, and anyone evaluating evidence integrity. **Fix:** create an artifact mapping manifest before prototyping. For MSK, either land the exact thumbnail into an existing truthful map position using the same source file, end continuity at the case-study header edge without pretending it became the workflow map, or disable MSK continuity. Never morph or narratively substitute the location crop for `MSKWorkflowMap`. **Verify:** 4/5 identify it as the same source and 0/5 believe it became workflow evidence.

2. **Focus/hover prefetch creates a keyboard and data asymmetry.** Keyboard users may download nonessential media merely by tabbing through the project list; pointer users do so by hovering. Save-Data protection helps but does not cover every constrained connection or user preference. **Fix:** do not fetch video on focus. Make the deterministic CSS/shared-artifact continuity the complete enhanced interaction; use the Higgsfield plate only when already decoded after a safe, post-load network check, and otherwise take the ordinary route. A visible focus state must never be coupled to a download. **Verify:** a keyboard pass through all three links causes no generated-media request.

3. **The route and focus contract needs measurable semantics.** “Focus lands according to the existing SPA route contract” is not enough for a cross-route overlay. Screen-reader users need the new page title and heading, while browser-back users need focus and scroll restored to the originating row. This affects screen-reader, keyboard, switch, voice, zoom, and interrupted users. **Fix:** specify and test document-title update, destination main/heading focus without an extra tab stop, overlay `aria-hidden`/unfocusable behavior, origin-row restoration on back, second-activation cancellation, and no reverse playback. **Verify:** VoiceOver and NVDA route announcement plus keyboard forward/back at 100%, 200%, and 400% zoom.

4. **The cold-load contract contains an unresolved product tension.** A 220–400KB plate requested only on activation cannot reliably decode inside a 560ms effect while also never delaying navigation. If late decode usually cuts to the page, the signature may disappear for first-time recruiters; if the route waits, it becomes loading. **Fix:** measure a CSS-only continuity baseline first and accept it as the cold-load experience. Generated texture may enhance only already-ready transitions. Do not preload all projects or hold the route to improve effect hit rate. **Verify:** cache-cold mid-range mobile title time is within 100ms of current; late decode always cuts cleanly.

5. **The paid-generation cap contradicts the scout.** Four low-resolution attempts plus one refinement exceeds the approved three-generation proof ceiling. **Fix:** three paid generations total: one block, one corrected block, one optional refinement. Stop after the third regardless of quality; record displayed credit cost and current entitlements before each run.

6. **Shared-element continuity is not original by itself.** The concept will read as authored only if each project-specific material cut adds meaning without becoming a reusable transition preset. This affects design leaders who have seen many polished portfolio transitions. **Fix:** test the route with no generated plate first, then add one MSK plate. Proceed only if the plate improves deliberate/connected perception or delayed recall without increasing loading or AI-demo responses. Preference and “smooth” comments do not count.

### Minor issues

1. **Screen-reader equivalence should remain semantic, not simulated.** The proposal correctly admits that optical surprise cannot be reproduced nonvisually. Do not add narration such as “animation playing” or decorative audio to manufacture parity. Preserve the same project name, qualifier, destination, heading, and evidence order; test task and meaning equivalence.

2. **The making-of line is optional but must not become claim repair.** `Product and evidence imagery remains unaltered` is useful secondary provenance, but the visible experience must already make ownership and status clear. Keep it in a concise case-study production note, not the homepage or transition.

### What works well

- The native project link is the only action; keyboard, touch, switch, and voice activation share the same route.
- No motion plays during the homepage scan, and reduced motion loads no generated media.
- Route priority, cancellation, late-decode cutoff, second activation, browser back, and media removal are considered rather than treated as edge cases.
- The pixel-level generative/deterministic boundary is exact and inspectable.
- Mobbin attribution and Grove Phase 2 status remain live and outside generated media.
- The concept is the furthest from poster, player, dashboard, explainer, or persistent AI spectacle.

## Required proof before paid generation

Use one shared prototype and test order so the concepts compete on outcomes rather than presentation quality:

1. Build **current**, **A static**, **A simulated motion**, **B CSS-only**, and **B simulated material-cut** states with existing artifacts. No Higgsfield credits.
2. Randomize current/A/B for five hiring-side participants; include at least one keyboard-only user and one motion-sensitive user. This small sample is a concept gate, not representative accessibility validation.
3. Test 390px and 1440px, then run technical locks at 320px, 200%, 400%, Spanish, reduced motion, forced colours, Save-Data, no JavaScript, media failure, cache-cold mobile, orientation change, and browser back.
4. Require for either concept: 5/5 open the intended project without instruction; 5/5 name it; 0/5 infer altered UI, fabricated evidence, inflated ownership, or shipped Grove status; readable title no more than 100ms slower than current.
5. Require for A: at least 4/5 distinguish the three projects for a real project reason, no discomfort, and a measurable recall gain over current rather than only “looks nice.”
6. Require for B: at least 4/5 identify the carried source as the same object, 0/5 call it loading, and correct route/focus recovery in keyboard plus VoiceOver/NVDA checks.
7. Only the winning grammar receives a maximum of **three paid generations** for one MSK plate. A paid result cannot rescue a weak no-credit interaction.

## Final recommendation

**Present both to Hillary as a real owner choice, with B recommended and the redlines visible.** A offers the more immediate homepage “how did she do that?” potential, but it is high-variance and can easily become expensive atmospheric spectacle. B is less screenshot-obvious but more defensible as product judgment: the surprise is caused by an ordinary user choice, disappears quickly, and leaves the work in control.

If Hillary chooses A, approve only the static/MSK no-credit prototype. If she chooses B, approve only the artifact-mapping manifest and CSS route prototype. In either case, paid generation remains blocked until the hard gates above pass.
