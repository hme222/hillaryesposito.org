The strict generic tasteskill score is capped at **6.5** because its house rules ban devices this portfolio intentionally uses: floating pill navigation, eyebrow/kicker labels, monospace captions, gradients, shadows, hairlines, and decorative numbering. Fixing isolated instances would create inconsistency. If this is revisited, do it as a deliberate full-system art-direction pass after the application cycle, not as a one-page polish sprint.
