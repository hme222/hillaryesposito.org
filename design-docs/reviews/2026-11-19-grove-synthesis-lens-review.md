# FloraGrove visuals through the Shape of AI "Synthesis" lens (2026-11-19)

Lens: https://www.shapeof.ai/patterns/synthesis. Synthesis combines many sources and restructures them into patterns or themes, which adds interpretation beyond a summary. Its four design rules: keep reasoning visible; separate evidence from interpretation; expose uncertainty; let the reader verify key conclusions. It names four kinds: aggregated, comparative, thematic, generative.

The pattern is written for AI products. FloraGrove has AI features but the page is not one, so the lens is used as a trust test: the survey ranking bars, the planned-changes ledger, the phase indicator and the prototype video are Hillary's own synthesis of research. The four rules decide whether a reader can tell what was observed (the five-person test, the survey), what is a ranking of stated preferences, what is planned and not built, and what is speculation. The survey ranking is judged as an aggregated/thematic synthesis.

Study: FloraGrove, route `/case-study/grove` (`RisoGrove.tsx`, `components/riso/*`), the pre-launch rebuild.
Seen live at 1440 x 1000 and 390 x 844 (mobile, 2x): hero with prototype video and waitlist link, "The first version tried to do too much", survey section with the top-three bars, the opened "See the rest of the survey ranking" disclosure, "The redesign is planned" section with the phase indicator, the three-column ledger and the opened "What the phases mean" note. The 93-second video was sampled every 6 seconds (16 frames). The Spanish page was loaded to check whether its visuals differ.

Smallest rendered text: the page has no SVG text (the one SVG on the page carries no labels). The smallest HTML text is 13 px at both 1440 and 390 (the "Pre-launch" eyebrow, the percentages, the Core/Later badges), which meets the site's 13 px `--ts-label` floor. The only text under the floor is inside the video itself (gap 9).

## What already works

- **The survey's evidence base sits on the visual, not behind it.** The paragraph right above the bars gives n (37), who took part (31 plant owners and caretakers, three florists, three bouquet buyers), the dates (May 22 to October 16) and the question ("Each picked the three features the app could not launch without"). This is the "Built from" line the MSK blueprint was missing.
- **Uncertainty is stated next to the data, in the same type as the claim.** "Limit: a survey tells you what people say they want... it does not prove what people would actually do" sits directly under the three bars at both widths. The test section has its own: "Limit: the test sessions showed confusion, but I did not keep notes on each task." Both are honest about the evidence, not hedges.
- **The bars are drawn to an honest scale.** Each track is 100% of the 37 people, so the 76% bar is visibly about three quarters full, and the nine "Later" rows are greyed, not hidden. Opening the disclosure gives the full twelve-row ranking, so a reader can check that the cut was the top three and that nothing above 49% was dropped.
- **Planned is separated from built in words, twice.** "These are plans for the next prototype, not finished screens" sits above the ledger and "Not built yet: the polished redesign, screens for empty or error moments, and a second round of testing" sits below it. The hero says "Pre-launch: nothing here has shipped." No visual on the page shows a finished redesigned screen.
- **The video is labeled as a prototype with made-up data in three places**: the hero note ("The video uses made-up sample data. It has no sound and loops."), the button ("Pause prototype video") and the video's accessible name, which also lists what happens in it. The sample account in frame (`maya@example.com`) is visibly fake.
- **Legibility on the HTML visuals holds at a phone width.** At 390 the ranking rows restack (label and percentage on one line, bar and badge on the next) and nothing goes below 13 px.

## Gaps

1. **The video does not say which version of the app it shows, and what it shows conflicts with the text (high).** The research section says the first version "opened on a feed and a mission, not on your own plants", and the ledger plans to "swap the pushy missions and feed" and "remove scorekeeping". The video opens on sign-up and a quiz, shows a screen titled "Track care, not metrics" with "FloraGrove does not gamify your plants", lesson cards with "Sources for this lesson", and Pip's nook. No feed and no mission appear in any sampled frame. So a reader cannot tell whether the video is the first version the five people tested, the current Phase 2 prototype, or part of the planned redesign, and parts of it look like the planned changes already exist, which the page says are "not finished screens". Separately, Pip's nook shows "Next in your nook: the shelf. Nothing is unlocked yet", an unlock track that a reader could read as the scorekeeping the ledger says to remove. Also, none of the three features the survey ranked top (reminders, camera plant ID, photo diagnosis) appears in the video; "Add a plant" is search by name. Fix: a one-line caption under the video naming the build and when it was recorded, and one line on how it relates to the version tested and the planned redesign. **Needs Hillary's input** (which build, recording date, whether the onboarding and lesson screens came before or after the five-person test).

2. **The percentages do not say what they are a percentage of (high).** The column reads "76%", "54%", "49%" with no unit. Because each person picked three, the twelve percentages add up to about 300%, and a reader who assumes "share of votes" will misread every bar. The paragraph says 37 people picked three, but the bars never connect to it. Fix: a column header or caption on the bars such as "share of the 37 people who picked it", and either the count beside each percentage or a note that every person picked three. The counts follow from the page's own numbers (76% of 37 is 28, the figure already used for reminders elsewhere), but **Hillary should confirm each count against the survey export** before it goes on the page, since the code comment says the percentages were rounded from the 37-response recompute.

3. **The planned changes are not traced back to the evidence, and one top-three feature disappears (medium).** The ledger lists Reminders, Plant ID by camera, and "Personality, not points". AI photo diagnosis, ranked third and tagged "Core" a scroll above, has no plan and no note saying why. "Personality, not points" is not one of the twelve survey features at all, so its source (presumably the five-person test, where people "got stuck" on the feed and mission) is left for the reader to guess. The Plant ID plan ("the top guesses, how sure it is, and its sources") reads like an answer to a survey question about trusting AI that the page never shows. This is the "keep reasoning visible" rule: a reader sees three conclusions but not which evidence each rests on. Fix: a small "From:" tag on each ledger column (the test, the survey ranking) and one line on where AI photo diagnosis went. **Needs Hillary's input** for the sources and for the diagnosis decision; do not add the AI-trust survey numbers unless she chooses to.

4. **The reason the cut falls at three is visible only after opening the disclosure (medium).** "The top three made the cut" rests on two things: each person picked three, and there is a steep drop after third place (49% to 22%). The drop is the strongest evidence for the cut, but the 22% rows are inside "See the rest of the survey ranking", closed by default. With it closed, the cut reads as a rule of thumb. Fix: show the fourth row (22%, already on the page) below a divider under the top three, or one line naming the drop from 49% to 22%. Also, "Twelve features became three" (in the survey paragraph and the "What I found" line) states as a finding what was Hillary's decision based on the ranking; the "Core" and "Later" badges sit in the data row in the same style as the percentages. A heading like the existing "The top three made the cut" already frames it as a choice; keep the badges, but they are interpretation, not data.

5. **The "made-up sample data" label is not attached to the video (medium).** At 1440 the note sits at the bottom of the left text panel, about 370 px left of the phone frame; the only text touching the video is the Pause button. At 390 the note sits above the phone, inside the text panel, with a panel edge and a gap between them. A reader who scrolls to the video or lands mid-page sees an app with a named account and a "Healthy" plant status and no label. Fix: move or repeat the note as the video's `figcaption` directly under the frame (the component is already a `figure`).

6. **The phase indicator shows Phase 2 as done (low-medium).** `PhaseIndicator` fills every segment below `current`, so segments one and two are both solid coral and the third is a thin line. The note defines "Phase 2: the decisions on this page", and the page says the decisions are plans not yet tested, so Phase 2 reads as finished when the text says "it works, but it isn't finished". Fix: draw the current segment as in progress (outline or half fill) distinct from completed ones, keeping the "Phase 2 of 3" text.

7. **Who answered is stated, but not whether their answers were pooled (low).** Six of 37 respondents are florists or bouquet buyers, and two of the twelve features are bouquet-only (bouquet scanner, florist QR pages), both near the bottom. A reader cannot tell whether the ranking pools all three groups or whether the plant owners alone would rank differently, nor how people were recruited. Fix: one line saying the ranking pools all 37, or the plant-owner-only order if it differs. **Needs Hillary's input** from the export.

8. **The five-person test is a claim with no observable trace (low).** "They got stuck right there" does not say whether that was all five or some, and the Limit line says no per-task notes were kept. The Limit is honest, so this is not a trust break, only an unclear quantifier. Fix: say how many of the five got stuck if Hillary remembers; otherwise leave as is. **Needs Hillary's input.**

9. **Text inside the video is far below the floor (low).** The video is a 780 px recording shown 286 px wide at 1440 and 148 px wide at 390. Its smaller UI text (for example "Step 1 of 3 · Your account", "We never share your details") renders at about 9 px on desktop and about 5 px on a phone (estimated from frame pixels; it is raster video, so it cannot be measured like SVG text). The page text says what the video shows, so no claim depends on reading it, but a reader cannot verify the "made-up sample data" claim from the frames on a phone. Fix: a larger frame on phones, or rely on the caption from gap 5.

10. **The survey dates have no year (low).** "From May 22 to October 16" follows "From March to May 2026" in the test section, so the year is implied, not stated. Fix: add 2026 (a locked fact).

## Not judged

- Video playback, autoplay and reduced-motion behavior were not tested; the video was sampled every 6 seconds, so a short screen between samples could be missed (including a feed or mission screen, which would change gap 1).
- The waitlist page at floragrove.app (external) and dark mode were not reviewed.
- The "result sentence" block under the hero ("What I found / Twelve features became three.") was being added by another agent and was not judged as a component, only quoted in gap 4.
- The settings menu visible in some desktop screenshots was opened by the capture script, not a page defect.
- Spanish: the page has no visuals at all (no video, no bars, no phase indicator; a stat grid and bullet lists only), so the visual checks do not apply. The same text issues carry over: the three percentage bullets have no "of 37" and come after the Limit line, the nine "Later" features are not listed, and the planned-change bullets have no source. Fixes to gaps 2, 3 and 10 should be mirrored there.
