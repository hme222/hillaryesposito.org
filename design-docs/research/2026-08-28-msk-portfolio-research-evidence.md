# MSK portfolio research evidence

## Portfolio Research Evidence Record

- Decision at stake: How to replace a paper-to-digital filing workaround with a clearer electronic medical-record workflow across four departments.
- Research mode: Descriptive and generative workflow research in the course of frontline operations.
- Research question: Where did the paper handoff, ownership changes, and system boundaries create avoidable filing work, and what future-state flow could remove that detour?
- Method and why it fit: Frontline workflow observation, process mapping, and stakeholder discussion were appropriate because the problem lived across handoffs and permissions rather than inside one screen.
- Participants or data source: Staff workflows across four Memorial Sloan Kettering departments. Exact participant and observation counts do not survive in the available record and are unknown.
- Date and product stage: During the 2018–2022 Administrative Assistant / Office Coordinator role; exact study dates are unknown. The redesign was initiated and presented before implementation, which occurred two roles later.
- Attitudinal / behavioral: Primarily behavioral observation of work as performed, with stakeholder input. No prevalence claim is made.
- Qualitative / quantitative: Primarily qualitative workflow evidence. Later operational outcome figures come from the broader implemented initiative, not a controlled study of this design alone.
- What was observed: A record moved through a four-system paper workaround before returning to the online chart. Ownership and the next filing action were obscured by the handoffs.
- What changed because of it: The future-state workflow replaced the detour with a filing queue and clearer ownership. MSK later implemented the redesign within a larger initiative, and the workflow remained in use through two system upgrades.
- Limitation or confidence boundary: Exact observation counts, interview notes, and original artifacts are unavailable. The 20% electronic medical-record cost reduction belongs to the organization-wide initiative; this workflow contributed within it and is not claimed as the sole cause. Public artifacts are recreated and contain no patient data.
- Artifact or source path: `design-docs/media/2026-07-29-evidence-copy.md`; `my-app/src/pages/case-studies/RisoMSK.tsx`; `my-app/src/components/MSKDashboardMockup.tsx`; public recreated case-study artifacts.
- Outcome status: Implemented and observed in continued use through two system upgrades; contribution to the larger cost outcome remains explicitly qualified.
