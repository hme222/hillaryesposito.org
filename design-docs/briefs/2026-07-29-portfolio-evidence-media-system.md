# Design Brief: Portfolio Evidence Media System

**Approved:** 2026-07-29 by Hillary Esposito

## Problem Statement

The portfolio has strong evidence and distinctive art direction, but its highest-value decisions still require several minutes of reading. Create a small media system that helps a recruiter understand the problem, judgment, and truthful outcome or status in seconds without converting the portfolio into an autoplay showreel.

## Users

- Recruiters scanning for role, credibility, and a reason to continue.
- Product and design hiring managers evaluating judgment, systems thinking, and craft.
- Readers using keyboard, screen reader, zoom, reduced motion, low-vision settings, or English as an additional language.
- Readers under time pressure, interruption, or cognitive load who need a static, resumable equivalent.

## Design Direction

Use an evidence-first maker/judge team:

1. Create one Grove flagship decision-trace micro-film and its static poster.
2. Prepare matching static evidence posters for MSK and Mobbin.
3. Produce additional micro-films only after the first asset passes truth, recruiter-value, accessibility, and taste review.

The shared visual language is a quiet editorial evidence plate: warm paper, restrained painted-cartography texture, real artifacts, typography-led hierarchy, and one coral signal. Motion communicates a decision transition rather than adding atmosphere.

## Asset Priority

1. **Grove decision trace:** AI built wide → research narrowed the product → human judgment overruled the risky defaults.
2. **MSK evidence poster:** four systems became two, using only recreated/anonymized workflow evidence.
3. **Mobbin capture-desk poster:** capture → map → name → verify, using the real third-party app captures and explicit ownership boundaries.

## Constraints

- No fabricated shipped outcomes, synthetic research participants, fake patient data, generated headshot, or invented finished Grove screens.
- Generated or reconstructed material must be visibly labeled.
- No autoplay, ambient loops, motion-only meaning, or audio dependency.
- Every film requires a poster frame, captions or equivalent visible copy, user control, and a reduced-motion/static alternative.
- Protect the existing three-case-study scan path and avoid adding another persistent control.
- Use current design tokens and page composition rather than creating a disconnected visual system.
- Preflight Higgsfield cost before paid generation; creator confidence is not evaluator evidence.

## Taste Direction

Quiet authority; warm restraint; organic material inside a disciplined grid; typography carries hierarchy; one expressive moment; craft felt rather than announced. Avoid glossy AI spectacle, generic cinematic trailers, synthetic fashion imagery, and decorative motion.

## Success Criteria

- A recruiter can state each featured project's problem, consequential decision, and truthful outcome/status after viewing the poster or a film no longer than eight seconds.
- The generated asset preserves all attribution and prototype-status qualifiers.
- The static poster communicates the same meaning without motion.
- No legible product UI is hallucinated or misrepresented.
- The asset passes desktop/mobile cropping, reduced-motion, keyboard, caption, contrast, and loading checks before site integration.
- The independent judge recommends `Use` or `Revise`, never producer self-approval.

## Out of Scope

- A personal brand reel, AI talking head, synthetic portrait, background music, social-media ad campaign, full case-study replacement, or deployment.
- Generating MSK patient-facing screens or a finished Grove product.

## Evaluation Matrix Check

- **Agentic UX / Trust:** user-initiated playback, visible provenance, pause/replay, static recovery.
- **Enterprise Design Systems / Prototyping:** existing tokens and compositions, responsive variants, no hidden local visual fork.
- **UX Research / Strategy:** evidence-to-decision trace, explicit confidence and product-stage boundaries.
- **Prompting / Evals:** frozen source packet, independent judge, critical failure for fabricated evidence or inaccessible delivery.
- **Unresolved hard gates:** generation cost, first-asset visual fidelity, and representative recruiter comprehension remain to be validated.
