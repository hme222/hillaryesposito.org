# Taste Baseline for the Site Critique

Date: 2026-10-21
Merges `~/.designpowers/taste-profile.md` (2026-06-10) with `2026-07-24-painted-cartography-taste-addendum.md`. The user's call: the June profile plus the riso direction, and flag any clash.

## Emotional target

Quiet authority. A well-run command center designed by someone who cares about materials. Trust through deliberateness, not volume.

## Principles the critique grades against

1. **Warm restraint.** Earth tones, organic accents, generous whitespace. Nothing decorative without a job.
   _Test: remove any decoration; does the page lose meaning?_
2. **Typography does the heavy lifting.** Hierarchy comes from weight, size and spacing, not colour or ornament. Headlines fit one line or break at a phrase boundary (CLAUDE.md).
   _Test: squint; is the reading order still clear?_
3. **Art stays behind a reading clearing.** The painted-cartography imagery is texture and rhythm, never the content. Left-side negative space protects headlines.
   _Test: text-on-art contrast passes AA in light and dark._
4. **Craft you feel, not announce.** Two-layer shadows, 1px borders at 6-10% opacity, radius 4/8/16, hover scale 1.01-1.02, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
5. **Specific to Hillary.** Not interchangeable with another portfolio.
   _Test: could this sentence or image sit on a stranger's site?_

## Quality level

Flagship.

## Colour

June profile: 85% warm neutrals, 12% olive/sage, 3% amber/gold.
Riso direction: eucalyptus paper `#E8ECE3`, structure inked grey-green, botanical inked pine green.

**Known tension to flag, not resolve silently:** the June profile says warm neutrals, the riso paper is a cool grey-green. Critics report which one a given page follows and whether pages disagree.

## Anti-references

Corporate template portfolios. Dribbble showcase excess (gradients, style over substance). Brutalist tech-bro minimal. Survey and GIS language: graticule ticks, compass, scale bar, plotted routes, registration targets.

## Voice (from `.designpowers/house-style/voice-spec.md`)

Calm, direct, first person for Hillary's own contribution. Claims name the project, the contribution, the observable output and any limitation. Banned: seamless, leverage, elevate, cutting-edge, game-changing, unlock. American English.

## Gen Z flair

One moment per project, subtle.
