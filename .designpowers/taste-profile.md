# Portfolio Taste Override

_Last updated: 2026-08-22 from Weekend Journal train exploration_

## Portfolio motion preference

- Prefer restrained Riso registration motion: a clean, recognizable silhouette, tactile ink misalignment, quiet lateral travel, and enough negative space for the editorial page to remain the protagonist.
- Use generated motion as a material layer, not as product evidence, navigation, or a realism showcase.
- For the Weekend Journal train, Exploration 01 is the approved art direction. Preserve its graphic restraint; do not drift toward painterly haze, literal realism, or dense collage noise in refinement.

## Positive evidence

Hillary selected the first of three Higgsfield/Seedance train explorations and responded **“1, LOVE.”** This is an emphatic single-project approval and should guide the production treatment without yet becoming a universal motion rule.

## Strong anti-pattern

- Do not turn AI-assisted production judgment into a named homepage framework, method explainer, control panel, or oversized interactive evidence diagram.
- Do not make the portfolio explain its own intelligence. Let the artifact, motion, and edit demonstrate judgment; keep production governance secondary and concise.
- A deformable frame, visible range, endpoint buttons, repeated workflow copy, and long method disclosure still read as poster/interface scaffolding even when technically interactive.
- The homepage must not become longer or more instructional to prove innovation.

## Evidence

Hillary's explicit response to the implemented Frame of Intent checkpoint: **“i hate the frame of intent.”** The rendered failure combined a large system name, duplicated source/decision copy, four registration controls, a range, endpoint buttons, and an expandable Higgsfield explanation. It announced the process instead of delivering high visual craft.

## Carry forward

- Preserve the original work and recruiter scan path as the protagonist.
- If Higgsfield returns, use it invisibly in the making: motion, camera, light, texture, composition, and editing around protected evidence.
- Production provenance may appear as a short secondary note inside the relevant case study, never as the homepage's main experience.
